**App name** (30 max)

RIPPL Studio: Fluid Art

**Short description** (80 max)

Paint with living fluid. Swirl color, light and particles with your fingers.

**Full description** (4000 max)

RIPPL Studio turns your screen into a pool of living color. Drag a finger and the fluid follows: it swirls, blooms and fades like ink in water, light in smoke, or a pocket-sized galaxy.

It's a toy, a sketchpad and a way to unwind, all in one.

PAINT WITH PHYSICS
• A real-time fluid simulation that reacts to every touch, with multi-touch
• 8 gesture modes: push, pull, swirl, attract, repel, inject, erase and vortex
• 14 scenes that move on their own: Whirlpool, Tornado, Fountain, Galaxy, Black Hole and more
• Gravity, buoyancy, turbulence, force fields and four kinds of screen edges

MAKE IT YOURS
• 11 color palettes, rainbow mode and automatic palette changes
• Bloom, sunrays, glow, lens flare, reflections and a blacklight mode
• Mirror and kaleidoscope symmetry
• GPU particles in 10 styles: sparks, bubbles, dust, orbs and more
• Text that pours color into the fluid, or stands as an obstacle in it
• Filters: film look, neon outlines, pixelate, motion echoes and more

KEEP AND SHARE
• Save your looks as presets and share them with friends
• Capture images at up to 1440p, straight to your gallery
• Hide the controls and let it run as a living display

BUILT FOR YOUR PHONE
• A touch-first interface that stays out of the way; the controls turn see-through while you adjust them
• Performance modes from Battery Saver to Maximum, with adaptive quality
• Works offline

NO ADS. NO ACCOUNTS. NO TRACKING.
RIPPL Studio collects no data at all. It's free and open source.

Based on WebGL Fluid Simulation by Pavel Dobryakov (MIT License).
